Retro 80s Icons TV — version 1.0.1

All 1,104 icon designs are centred horizontally and vertically, including
the newly recreated Apps generic icon. PNGs returned to Projectivy are also
centred at 320 x 180 pixels with a transparent background.

Install Retro-80s-Icons-TV-Centred.apk over the previous Retro 80s Icons app.
It uses the same package and signing identity, so no uninstall is required.
Open the app, choose Apply, then Apply in Projectivy and confirm.

For an individually chosen custom icon, select it again through the
launcher's icon picker if the launcher still shows a cached image.

Requires Android TV 9 or newer. All 1,103 other original designs retain
their exact source PNGs; Android positions their visible artwork centrally.
The requested appsgeneric replacement is also supplied as a separate PNG.

Verified: update installation, all 1,104 drawable outputs, 24 exported PNGs,
34 APK checks and 34 Android TV remote/Projectivy interaction checks.
Testing used an isolated Android TV emulator, not a physical TV.
