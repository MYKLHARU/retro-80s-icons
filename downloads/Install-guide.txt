Retro 80s Icons — Android TV

1,104 original retro icons, with a matching 80s interface, remote controls,
search, 12 categories, artwork previews and a random-icon button.

Requires Android TV 9 or newer. This is a separate app from Glitch Reflections.

Install Retro-80s-Icons-TV.apk on your Android TV, then open Retro 80s Icons.
Choose Apply > Apply in Projectivy and confirm the launcher's prompt.

Manual route in Projectivy:
Settings > Appearance > Cards > Icon pack > Retro 80s Icons.

To choose a particular icon, open the launcher's custom icon picker and
select Retro 80s Icons. The app will return the selected transparent PNG.

The supplied 320 x 180 PNG artwork is included without recolouring, resizing
or filters. Automatic app mappings cover the designs supplied in this pack;
other icons can be selected individually. Glitch Reflections remains separate.

The APK was checked in an isolated Android TV emulator. It has not been
installed or tested on your physical TV or Shield in this chat.
